LOCKPICK v0.12 UPDATE
=====================

Extract the UPDATE_ONLY zip directly into your existing Lockpick folder and overwrite program/readme files.
The update-only archive intentionally contains no data folder, so it cannot overwrite your newer checkpoints, demonstrations or audit history.

FULL archive:
Contains the data snapshot that was inside the Lockpick.zip supplied for this update. Use it for a clean separate install. Do not use the FULL archive over a newer actively-trained folder unless you intentionally want that older snapshot.
